Model notes

Every wall, door, window and fixture is entered in the pixel coordinates of the original floor plan and converted with one scale, so the model sits directly on the drawing.

Assumptions

  • The west door is the entry. The east door beside the living window is modelled as a glazed door.
  • Window sills at 0.5 m and heads at 2.35 m. The bath window is high and frosted.
  • Closet, laundry and entry doors are shown closed. Bedroom and bath doors stand open as drawn.
  • The furniture layout is my own proposal. The tour follows the seven stops of the supplied camera route, nudged where needed to clear this furniture.

Rendering limits

  • This is real-time WebGL, not an offline path-traced render. It is not photographic.
  • Bounce light is approximated with environment lighting. There is no true global illumination or ambient occlusion pass.
  • Sun and four room lights cast shadows. Lamps and accent strips do not.
  • The bath mirror reflects the room. Other glossy surfaces reflect a generic interior.
  • Materials are generated in code. The view outside is a painted backdrop.
